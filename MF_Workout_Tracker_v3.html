HOW TO READ THIS PLAN

▼

4 phases. Each phase is 6 weeks. You train 3 days per week (Phase 4 is 4 days). You always alternate Workout A and Workout B each session.

TermWhat it means
SetsHow many rounds of an exercise. 3 sets = you do it 3 times with rest between each.
RepsHow many times you lift in one set. 6-8 reps = lift 6 to 8 times before resting.
SupersetTwo exercises done back to back with no rest between them. Rest after both are done.
Progressive OverloadAdding a small amount of weight or doing one more rep each session. This is how muscle and strength are built.
DeloadA planned easy week after 6 weeks of hard training. You use 50% weight. Cannot be skipped.
AlternateSwitch between two exercises each session. Session 1 do Deadlift. Session 2 do Squats. Session 3 back to Deadlift.
Every session: do one more rep or add a small amount of weight vs last time.
Log every workout. Write the exercise, weight, sets, and reps. This is non-negotiable.
Soreness does NOT mean growth. Getting stronger means growth.
Missing a session is fine. Do not double up. Just continue where you left off.

DELOAD WEEK: WHAT IT IS AND HOW TO DO IT

▼

After 6 weeks of training, your joints, tendons, and nervous system are tired even if you do not feel it. A deload week is a planned easy week at 50% of your normal weight. It lets everything fully recover before the next phase.

Deload weeks are required. You cannot skip them. The program FAQ is clear on this.
What changes during deloadWhat stays the same
Reduce weight to 50% of what you were liftingSame exercises from the phase just finished
Reduce to 2-3 sets per exerciseSame rep ranges
Stop well before failure on every setSame rest periods
Session will feel very easy. That is the goal.Same workout days (MWF)

Example: If you were floor pressing 60kg for 4 sets of 6-8 reps in Phase 1, your deload session is 30kg for 2-3 sets of 6-8 reps. It should feel very easy.

Deload schedule: Week 7 (after Phase 1)  |  Week 14 (after Phase 2)  |  Week 21 (after Phase 3)

HOW TO PROGRESS: THE 2-STEP METHOD

▼
StepWhat you doExample
Step 1Hit the TOP of the rep range across ALL sets first.Target 6-8 reps. Complete 8 reps on all 4 sets before moving to Step 2.
Step 2Next session, add the smallest weight available.You hit 4×8 last time. Add 2.5kg. Aim for 6 reps again. Work back up to 8.
Start lighter than you think you need to. Week 1 should feel too easy.
Add 2.5kg at a time. Never make big jumps.
If you cannot hit the minimum reps, the weight is too heavy. Lower it.
Weeks 1-2: focus on form only. Week 3 onward: start adding weight.

AB EXERCISES

▼

Every workout ends with an abs superset. Pick any 2 exercises, do them back to back with no rest. That is 1 superset. Rest 1-1.5 min. Repeat 3 times total. In Phase 3 and 4, pick 3 exercises per superset.

PHASE 1 SUBSTITUTES

If you cannot do...

  • Floor Press → Wide Push-Ups / Weighted Push-Ups
  • Overhead Press → Arnold Press / Pike Push-Ups
  • Close Grip Floor Press → Dips / DB Tricep Extension
  • Deadlift → Romanian DL / Sumo DL / DB Deadlift
  • Goblet Squat → Lunges / Step-Ups / Split Squat
  • Pull-Ups → Band-Assisted / Negative Pull-Ups
  • Chin-Ups → Underhand Cable Row / DB Row
  • Barbell Row → 1-Arm DB Row / Inverted Row
  • DB Curl → Barbell Curl / Hammer Curl / EZ Bar

Rest periods

  • Main lifts: 2-3 minutes
  • Deadlifts and Squats: 3-5 minutes
  • Abs and Calves: 1-1.5 minutes
  • Be consistent. Same rest time every set.
  • Weeks 1-2: focus on form only, not weight
  • Week 3 onward: start adding weight

KEY RULES

▼
RuleWhy it matters
Stay in each phase for the full 6 weeks minimum.Skipping ahead means missing the strength base the next phase requires.
Do NOT skip the deload week between phases.Your joints and nervous system need it. You will lift more after a deload.
Meet ALL strength requirements before the next phase.If you cannot meet them, you are not ready. Stay and keep building.
Log every single workout.You cannot track progress you did not write down.
Add weight in small increments only.Small consistent jumps build more total strength over 28 weeks.
Do NOT add extra sets or exercises.Extra volume as a beginner causes overtraining and stalled progress.
If cutting: reduce 5-set exercises to 4 sets and 4-set exercises to 3 sets.Lower recovery capacity when in a calorie deficit.
Track calories in MyFitnessPal every day.Nutrition is equally important as training.
Take 5g creatine monohydrate every day. No loading phase needed.Most researched supplement for strength and muscle. It works.
Sleep 7-9 hours. Drink 2-3 liters of water daily.Muscle is built during recovery, not during training.
Not sore after a workout? Normal. Soreness is not a measure of growth.
Program not working? Ask: Am I getting stronger? Am I logging? Am I eating enough protein? Fix those three first.
Muscles look smaller than before? Normal when switching from pump training. Muscle density is increasing.

KETTLEBELL TESTS

▼

Snatch Test

100 reps in 5 min. Switch hands as needed. The classic KB benchmark.

TGU Test

Half-bodyweight TGU, 1 rep each side, clean form. The total-body strength gate.

Goblet Squat Hold

20kg held at chest, full depth, 1 minute. Pure leg + core endurance.

Farmer Carry

Hold 2× bodyweight (combined). Walk for distance. Grip + posterior chain test.

BODYWEIGHT TESTS

▼

Max Pull-Ups

Strict, dead-hang to chin-over-bar. One unbroken set.

Max Push-Ups (2 min)

As many strict reps as possible in 2 minutes. Rest allowed in plank position.

Max Dips

Parallel bar, full depth (shoulders below elbows), lock out at top.

Pistol Squat

Max consecutive reps per leg. Full depth, controlled descent.
